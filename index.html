How to use

  • Tap Add to add someone. Type a name, or pick them from Contacts (the system picker; the app never reads your other contacts).
  • When they mention something they want, tap + next to their name, type it and tap Done.
  • Shopping in Safari or an app? Tap Share › Gift Ideas and tap a name. The title and price are filled in from what you shared; nothing is fetched from the web.
  • Tap the circle when you buy it. After the birthday or holiday it moves to Given, by year.
  • Reminders: set birthdays, anniversaries and holidays per person; choose how many days before in Settings.
  • Send to family: share a person’s list as a code. They paste it in Add › Got a code from family.

Purchase

Free: up to 3 people. Everything is open for the first 30 days. One non-consumable purchase unlocks unlimited people. No subscription. Restore it in Settings › Unlock everything › Restore purchase.

Privacy

Gift Ideas does not collect any data. Your lists stay on your iPhone (shared only with the app’s own share extension). No account, no analytics, no ads, no server.
